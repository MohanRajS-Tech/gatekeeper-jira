# Support and Service Level Agreement (SLA) Policy

**Vendor:** TheOpsLabs  
**Product:** Gatekeeper for Jira  
**Effective Date:** October 16, 2026  
**Website:** https://theopslabs.com  
**Support Email:** support@theopslabs.com  

---

## 1. Hours of Operation
* **Standard Business Hours:** Monday through Friday, 09:00 – 17:00 IST (UTC+05:30).
* **Exclusions:** Weekends (Saturday and Sunday) and Indian national holidays. Support inquiries received outside business hours will be queued for the next immediate business day.

---

## 2. Support Channels
Users and administrators may initiate support requests via:
* **Email:** support@theopslabs.com
* **Issue Tracker & Community Support:** https://github.com/MohanRajS-Tech/gatekeeper-jira/issues
* **Atlassian Marketplace:** Via the "Contact Us" or "Get Support" button on the official app listing.

---

## 3. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Response Times
TheOpsLabs commits to the following initial response targets within operational business hours:

| Severity Level | Definition | Target Initial Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical (L1)** | Complete outage, workflow transition completely blocked preventing critical work. | **Within 24 calendar hours** (Atlassian mandated) |
| **Major (L2)** | Significant feature malfunction with no immediate native Jira workaround. | **Within 12 business hours** |
| **Normal / Minor (L3)** | General inquiries, setup guidance, feature suggestions, or documentation clarification. | **Within 24 business hours** |

---

## 4. Resolution Protocol & Maintenance
* **Cloud Architecture:** Gatekeeper runs 100% serverless on Atlassian Forge inside Atlassian's secure cloud perimeter. 
* **Uptime & Availability:** Availability is governed by Atlassian Cloud platform infrastructure.
* **Patches:** Fixes and compatibility updates are deployed seamlessly through Forge continuous delivery without scheduled customer downtime.
